# Thoughts of Cee — First 30-Day Content Operating Plan

**Primary objective:** establish a recognisable publishing rhythm, learn what genuinely resonates, and grow a relevant audience without compromising the brand's voice or emotional integrity.

**Primary channel:** Instagram (@thoughtsofceee). Adapt approved work for Facebook and TikTok rather than creating three unrelated content calendars. Re-check each platform's current creator guidance and account analytics.

## Brand position

Thoughts of Cee is a quiet, honest, literary space for people who want to feel recognised through words and stories. It is not a therapy service, motivational account, or generic quote page.

Voice: warm, reflective, human, conversational, understated and emotionally intelligent.

Visual language: vintage editorial, warm linen (#EAE4DB), black (#000000), soft text (#292522), muted brown (#765D4B), paper-line (#CFC4B7), literary details, recurring objects, restrained typography and cinematic photography.

Preferred formats:
- Photographs paired with conversational storytelling.
- Continuous-talk carousels.
- Real-voice photo sequences.
- Short writing extracts and fragments.
- Short cinematic reels with original voiceover where appropriate.

## Publishing rhythm

Initial target, based on the previously discussed growth plan:
- 3 Reels per week.
- 2 carousel or image-led posts per week.
- 10 genuine audience interactions per day (thoughtful replies and comments, not engagement bait).
- 1 collaboration per week where a relevant, values-aligned partner is available.

This is a test cadence, not a universal algorithm rule. If quality or sustainability drops, reduce volume and protect consistency. Reuse core creative assets across platforms with native adaptations; do not simply watermark and repost identical exports.

## Content pillars and recurring series

1. FRAGMENTS — short, recognisable observations; useful for discovery and sharing.
2. LETTERS — longer reflections with a beginning, emotional progression and thoughtful ending.
3. READ THAT AGAIN — conversational carousel built around one unfolding idea.
4. THE PUSH — gentle perspective shifts or invitations to consider something differently; never preachy.
5. DAYS BETWEEN / THINGS I LEARNED SLOWLY / LOVE & LOSS — thematic collections for deeper editorial work.

## Weekly workflow

- Monday: Audience & Storytelling Agent proposes 5 briefs using audience signals, brand themes and evidence.
- Tuesday: Editorial Agent drafts and Creative Director develops visual direction.
- Wednesday: Founder reviews and approves the week's content.
- Thursday–Sunday: Distribution Agent adapts and schedules approved posts; community interactions are handled thoughtfully.
- Weekly review: The Brain reports what was published, what resonated, what did not, and what to test next.

No post is published automatically without founder approval.

## Week 1 starter calendar

### Monday — Reel / FRAGMENTS
**Working theme:** The quiet work nobody sees  
**Creative direction:** cinematic, unhurried everyday details; restrained on-screen typography; original voiceover if approved.  
**Opening thought:** "Not every change announces itself."  
**Narrative:** the small private choices that gradually change how a person lives.  
**Ending:** leave a reflective pause; no demand to comment or share.

### Tuesday — Carousel / READ THAT AGAIN
**Working theme:** You can miss something and still know it was not right for you.  
**Format:** continuous-talk carousel, one connected thought across slides, not disconnected quote cards.  
**Sensitivity:** avoid telling people what to do about a relationship; keep it reflective rather than prescriptive.

### Wednesday — Reel / LETTERS
**Working theme:** A letter to the version of me who kept waiting.  
**Creative direction:** paper, handwritten detail, quiet domestic imagery, voice-led pacing.  
**Narrative:** acknowledge waiting, the meaning it held, and the possibility of moving forward without shaming the past self.

### Friday — Photo-led post / THE PUSH
**Working theme:** What if you stopped measuring your progress against the person you were last year?  
**Format:** one strong editorial photograph with a conversational caption.  
**Tone:** gentle perspective shift, not motivational instruction.

### Sunday — Reel / DAYS BETWEEN
**Working theme:** The days when nothing seems to happen, but something is changing.  
**Creative direction:** a real-voice photo sequence or short cinematic montage.  
**Narrative:** the invisible middle between an ending and a beginning.

## Week 2–4 themes

Rotate the series while avoiding repetition:
- The things we learn to say too late.
- The difference between being understood and being agreed with.
- The quiet ways people carry change.
- A letter to the part of you that is tired of explaining.
- The ordinary rituals that help a day feel possible.
- What we leave unsaid, and why.
- Learning to be honest without being cruel to ourselves.
- When a chapter ends without a clear ending.

These are prompts, not finished claims about psychology. The Editorial Agent must make each piece original and grounded in the brand's own writing.

## Community practice

Daily:
- Leave up to 10 thoughtful, relevant interactions with aligned creators and community members.
- Reply to genuine comments in the brand's voice.
- Do not solicit sensitive disclosures.
- Do not diagnose or provide counselling in DMs.
- Do not turn private messages into posts without permission.

Weekly:
- Explore one values-aligned collaboration.
- Ask one low-pressure audience question when appropriate.
- Capture recurring themes from public feedback without identifying individuals.

## Measurement

Record weekly:
- Posts published vs planned.
- Reach and non-follower reach.
- Average watch time / retention for video where available.
- Saves and shares per reach.
- Meaningful comments and profile visits.
- Follower growth and unfollows.
- Website visits and email signups.
- Which series and formats generated meaningful responses.

Do not judge a post by likes alone. Do not infer psychological impact from engagement metrics. Small samples are directional signals, not proof.

## 30-day success criteria

- Publish consistently at a sustainable quality level.
- Complete four weekly reviews.
- Identify the two strongest recurring formats by observed account data.
- Identify audience questions or themes worth developing.
- Build a reusable production workflow.
- Produce one documented learning report and a revised month-two plan.

Follower count is tracked, but the first month's primary success is a repeatable process and credible audience learning—not a promised follower number.

## Approval and safety

- Founder approves all posts before publication.
- No fabricated personal experiences or testimonials.
- No clinical or diagnostic claims.
- No manipulative hooks or emotional exploitation.
- Verify factual or psychological claims against credible sources.
- Credit third-party work and respect image, music and text rights.
- Escalate sensitive topics and crisis-related disclosures for human handling.
